```
A big update with a brand-new look!

• New game "Which one?": hear the name and find the right photo
• Three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard (with a timer)
• Player album: collect a card for every footballer you know

### Description

```
Football Kids Quiz is a colorful football quiz made for children. Look at the photo, guess the footballer, earn stars and fill your player album!

TWO GAMES
• Who is it? – Look at the photo and pick the right name.
• Which one? – Hear the name and find the right photo. Great for children who cannot read yet.

THREE LEVELS
• Easy – 2 choices
• Medium – 4 choices
• Hard – 4 choices against a 10-second timer

STARS, LEVELS AND AN ALBUM
• Earn up to 3 stars in every 10-question game
• Climb from Rookie to Legend
• Collect all 25 player cards in your album
• Beat your own best score in every game and level

SOUNDS THAT HELP LEARNING
• Every name is read aloud, in a voice that pronounces it properly
• Cheerful sound effects and gentle vibration
• Sound can be turned off with one tap

MADE FOR KIDS, TRUSTED BY PARENTS
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts and no data collection
• No internet connection needed
• No microphone, camera or location permission
• Settings and all external links are behind a parental gate

Available in English and Turkish, on iPhone and iPad.
```

## App Review Information

Sign-in required: **No** (the app has no accounts).

The notes follow the same seven-point structure as the 1.0 submission that was approved. Two things are yours to do before pasting: **record a new screen video on a physical device** and **fill in the physical device line in section 2** (marked `[FILL IN]`). Simulator lines list what was actually tested.

### Notes

```
Hello App Review Team,

Version 1.1 is a redesign of the offline kids quiz approved as 1.0.

1. Screen recording on physical device (latest OS)
- A screen recording from a physical iPhone is attached. It shows the full flow from launch:
Home screen: switch game ("Who is it?" / "Which one?") and level (Easy / Medium / Hard)
Play "Who is it?": answer questions, use the speaker button for pronunciation, see progress and score
Play "Which one?": hear the name, pick the photo
Hard level with the 10-second timer
Result screen with stars
Album with collected player cards

Settings behind the parental gate: sound, language (Phone / English / Turkish), reset album

- The app does NOT request any permission (location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, notifications) and does NOT use App Tracking Transparency.

2. Device models and OS versions tested before submission
- Simulator tests: iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.2), iPhone 17 Pro Max (iOS 26.2), iPad Pro 13-inch M5 (iPadOS 26.2), iPad A16 (iPadOS 18.6, landscape).
- Physical device testing: [FILL IN: device model and iOS version]

3. App functions and target audience
- App name: Football Kids Quiz
- Category/audience: Kids, early learners and families
- Core function: a football-player recognition quiz for children, with two games: see a photo and pick the name, or hear a name and pick the photo.
- Value provided: helps children learn football player names through visual matching, repetition and spoken names. Stars, levels and a card album reward progress.

4. Setup instructions / access to main features
- No account, login or sample files are required.
- Main flow: open the app, choose a game and a level, tap "Play", answer 10 questions (speaker icon for pronunciation), view stars and score, open "My Album" for collected cards.
- Settings (gear icon, top right) is for parents and opens through the parental gate described below.

5. External services, tools, platforms used for core functionality
- No external online service is used. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
- Core app stack: React Native + Expo framework.
- On-device modules: localization, text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer), sound-effect playback (AVAudioPlayer), haptics and local storage for progress.
- No third-party analytics, advertising or data-collecting SDKs.

6. Regional differences
- Functionality is the same in all regions; only the interface language differs (English / Turkish).

7. Regulated industry / protected third-party material

https://serhaton.github.io/football-kids-quiz/player_image_sources.html

- The Settings screen always opens through a mandatory parental gate.

- The gate is a 2-step adult task: press and hold for 2 seconds, then solve a randomized math challenge. If it is not completed, nothing opens. It cannot be disabled.

Privacy
- Data Not Collected. Progress and settings are stored only on the device.
- Privacy Policy (also linked in Settings): https://serhaton.github.io/football-kids-quiz/v1.1/privacy.html

Developer
