Frequently asked questions

  • What are the two games? Who is it? shows a photo and asks for the name. Which one? reads a name aloud and asks for the photo. Switch between them on the home screen.
  • What do Easy, Medium and Hard change? Easy has 2 choices, Medium has 4, and Hard has 4 choices with a 10-second timer.
  • How do I open Settings? Tap the gear on the home screen. Settings are for parents, so you first hold a button for 2 seconds and solve a short maths question.
  • How do I change the language? In Settings choose Türkçe, English or Phone language.
  • How does the album work? Every player your child answers correctly is added to the album as a card. Tap a card to hear the name.
  • How do I reset the album? Open Settings and choose “Reset album”, then confirm. Stars and best scores are kept.
  • Why is there no sound? Check the ring/silent switch and the volume, and make sure the speaker icon on the home screen is not crossed out.
  • Does it work without internet? Yes. The whole game works offline.
  • Are there ads or purchases? No. There are no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Device model (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air)
  • iOS or iPadOS version
  • App version (1.1.0)
  • What happened and what you expected
  • A screenshot or screen recording if possible
