Privacy Policy

Short version: Football Kids Quiz collects nothing. Everything stays on your device.

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1

1. Information we collect

None. Football Kids Quiz does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, device identifiers, photos or voice recordings.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the following is saved only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Stars earned and best scores
  • The player cards collected in the album
  • The chosen game, difficulty, language and sound setting

A parent can clear the album at any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes all of this data.

3. Permissions

The app does not ask for any device permission. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or notifications.

4. Internet access

The game is fully playable offline and does not contact any server. The only time the app opens the internet is when a parent chooses to open a link (this policy or an image source page) in the browser.

5. Ads, analytics and purchases

  • No advertising of any kind
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No in-app purchases
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

6. Children's privacy

Football Kids Quiz is made for children. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children. Settings and every link that leaves the app are protected by a parental gate (a two-second press followed by a maths question).

7. Photos in the game

The player photos come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses and are bundled inside the app. Sources and licenses are listed in the app and on the Image Sources & Licenses page.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to serhaton@gmail.com.
