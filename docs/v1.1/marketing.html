Football Kids Quiz

A colorful football quiz for kids. Guess the stars, earn stars and fill your player album.

⚽ Made for kids📴 Works offline🚫 No ads🔒 No data collected
Download on the App Store

Two games in one

  • Who is it? Look at the photo and pick the right name.
  • Which one? Hear the name and find the right photo – great for children who cannot read yet.
  • Short rounds of 10 questions.

Grows with your child

  • Easy: 2 choices
  • Medium: 4 choices
  • Hard: 4 choices against a 10-second timer

Stars, levels and an album

  • Earn up to 3 stars in every game
  • Climb from Rookie to Legend
  • Collect all 25 player cards in your album
  • Beat your own record in every mode

Sounds that help learning

  • Every name is read aloud in a voice that pronounces it properly
  • Cheerful sound effects and gentle vibration
  • One tap to turn sound off

For parents

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
  • No internet connection needed and no data collected
  • No microphone, camera or location permission
  • Settings and every link that leaves the app sit behind a parental gate
  • English and Turkish
