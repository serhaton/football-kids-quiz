Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Football Kids Quiz handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Football Kids Quiz is designed to work offline and does not require account creation. We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Data Usage

Gameplay data (such as score and language preference) is stored locally on your device only to support app functionality.

  • No data is sold to third parties.
  • No personal profile is created.
  • No analytics tracking is used for advertising.

3. Internet and Network Access

The app is intended to be fully playable offline. It does not need an internet connection for core gameplay.

4. Children's Privacy

Football Kids Quiz is a family-friendly game for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not provide behavior-based advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

Football Kids Quiz does not require third-party login or external social integrations for gameplay. Public image source and license details are available at Image Sources & Licenses.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
