Frequently Asked Questions

  • How can I change language? Open Settings in the app and choose English, Turkish, or Phone Language.
  • Does the app work offline? Yes, gameplay works fully offline.
  • Why is sound not playing? Please check your mute switch and volume level on the device.
  • How many questions are in one game? Each game has 10 questions.

When Reporting an Issue

Please include the following details in your email:

  • Device model (example: iPhone 15)
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • Short description of the issue
  • Screenshot or screen recording (if possible)
